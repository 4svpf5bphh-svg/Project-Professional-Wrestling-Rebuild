# Multi-person Individual matches — triple threats & four-ways
The agreed INDIVIDUAL match family now has two additional first-fall-wins
formats: 3-way and 4-way. They are not elimination formats or battle royals.

- Each participant is a single booked/contracted side A, B, C, or D.
  Scripted winning individual is unchanged by simulation except for the
  already approved severe medical stoppage rule.
- Scoring evaluates the full field, using the preserved archived PPW
  quality and audience equations across the three/four wrestler averages.
  Deterministic medical risks apply to *every* opponent, with a specified
  skill-ranked replacement winner if the scripted winner is incapacitated.
  Multi-person run-ins are blocked until their own 3/4-side model exists.
- A verified C/D winner persists in canonical completed shows, with matching
  evidence and no invented eliminations. Individual careers persist every
  participant's fatigue, wear, momentum, recognition, popularity and injury
  receipt in two bounded SQL writes. A precondition checks exact snapshots,
  so a duplicate or stale result never silently double-progresses a wrestler.
- Singles belts may be defended against several competitors: the reigning
  holder must be on the card, and a verified clean challenger win transfers
  the championship in the same transaction.
- The 1v1 kernel and 2v2 tag engine are preserved; unsupported 5-person,
  elimination, trios, 4v4, 5v5, Battle Royals remain gated, not faked.
- Distinct wrestlers may appear in only one career-mutating match per show
  in this first slice, until whole-show aggregation is enabled. Normal
  clearance still checks all participant contracts and service capacity.
- Do not claim measured Neon savings from local PostgreSQL query proxy
  figures; staging billed transfer/compute is a separate release gate.

No production deployment or World Lock cron.
