# PPW trusted AI / delegated staff commercial preparation

## October 2026 milestone
- The original Genesis importer preserves promotion financial caution and event cadence alongside the World's major-event interval.
- The existing server-only World stage for delegated staff fallback now also produces a real scheduled AI card for an explicitly AI-managed, unclaimed active promotion with no submitted draft.
- Staff-controlled promotions require recorded pre-lock delegation from their active owner. No unassigned or human-controlled promotion is automatically booked or has creative outcomes rewritten.
- AI/staff generated cards use deterministic World-seeded contracted, available same-division wrestlers and verified venue/day options. Contract dates and cash remain untouched until authoritative resolution.
- Before World resource clearance, a separate idempotent World command obtains the venue's market, chooses a ticket strategy via original financial-caution rules, and freezes expected demand and ticket yield from the submitted card. It fails closed on missing original inputs.
- One command is issued once per weekly stage. Incomplete player-authored commercial plans block with a clear reason. No online attendance polling or extra dashboard projection is introduced.
- New booking_origin AI schema separates human, delegated, and AI authority.
